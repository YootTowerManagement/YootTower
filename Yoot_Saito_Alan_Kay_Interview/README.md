## Online Reference

Brett Victor’s HTML edition of Alan Kay’s HOPL paper, [The Early History Of Smalltalk](https://worrydream.com/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk/), is the cleanest online reading version of the main paper. A local Markdown conversion is preserved here as [EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk.md](EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk.md), with its imported PNG files listed in [EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk-Import.md](EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk-Import.md).

Credit and thanks to [Brett Victor](https://worrydream.com/) for creating and publishing the cleaned-up HTML edition and accompanying image set in the [worrydream/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk](https://github.com/worrydream/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk) repository. The local `EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk-*` files are derived from that work.

The local appendix files preserve additional material around the MACWORLD Japan interview, page scans, and appendix images/transcripts that are not all visible in that online rendering.

## Why This Matters

This interview captures Yoot Saito engaging Alan Kay in a deep discussion of personal computing, Dynabook, Smalltalk, Xerox PARC, education, media, simulation, and the future of interactive systems.

It is also evidence of Yoot’s role as a bridge between Japanese game/media culture and foundational US computing research.
